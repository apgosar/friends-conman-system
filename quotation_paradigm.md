## 1. Project Overview
This quotation covers the licensing, one-time on-premise deployment, and ongoing maintenance of the **Neev CMS** for **Paradigm Friends Realtors LLP**. The system will be deployed on the client's own physical infrastructure and is licensed for the management of **2 Real Estate Projects** until their completion.

## 2. Scope of Work & Included Features
The Neev CMS suite provides end-to-end management of real estate bookings, financial tracking, and customer communications. The following features are included in this deployment:

### Project & Inventory Management
* Configuration of up to 2 Real Estate Projects.
* Management of Wings, Floors, and Units (Residential/Commercial).
* Real-time tracking of Unit Status (Available, Booked, Sold, Blocked).

### Booking & Sale Lifecycle
* Comprehensive buyer registration (Primary & Co-owners).
* KYC Document Management (PAN, Aadhaar) with secure local storage.
* Dynamic generation of Quotation, Allotment Letters, and Agreements for Sale.

### Financial Tracking & Milestones
* Custom Payment Schedules linked to Construction Milestones.
* Automated tracking of Stamp Duty, Registration, and Parking Charges.
* Payment collection tracking, receipt generation, and ledger maintenance.

### Automated Communications
* **Demand Letters:** Auto-generation and dispatch of demand letters upon milestone completion.
* **Architect Certificates:** Seamless attachment of architectural completion certificates with demand letters.
* **Omnichannel Delivery:** Automated delivery of all documents and payment receipts via **Email (Gmail SMTP)** and **WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API)**.

### Deployment & Security
* **On-Premise Setup:** Full installation on your local Windows Server infrastructure.
* **Zero-Trust Access:** Configuration of Cloudflare Tunnel to provide secure remote access to the CRM.
* **Data Sovereignty:** All data, including the database and customer documents, remains strictly on your local servers.
